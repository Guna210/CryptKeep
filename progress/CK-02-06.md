# CK-02-06 — Add sprint and delayed resource regeneration

**Builder/model:** `/root/ck_02_06_builder` / GPT-6 Luna
**Status:** **SUBMITTED** for independent review; not accepted or published.

## Result

Added Left Shift sprint to the actual fixed-tick `PlayerSession`, charging 18 stamina/second only after collision resolution confirms more than `1e-8 m` of valid displacement. Sprint uses 5.5 m/s; walking remains 3.5 m/s. Stationary/opposing input, secondary-held guard/ADS seam, inactive state, pure healing/stance restrictions, unaffordable full-tick cost, and fully blocked motion cannot sprint. Sliding movement remains valid. A fully blocked sprint candidate retries at walking speed and suppresses that tick's sprint charge. When sprint is no longer eligible, inherited planar velocity is immediately capped to walking speed.

Added independent simulation-time stamina and mana regeneration timers. Stamina restores at 22/second after 0.65 seconds without spending; mana restores at 6/second after 1 second without spending. A tick crossing a delay grants only the part after the boundary. Spending resets only its resource timer and prevents same-tick regeneration. Inactive ticks freeze resources and timers; no health regeneration occurs. `recordStaminaSpend(timers)` is the shared stamina-delay seam for a future accepted dash cost. Successful floor loads reset the resources through the existing initial player state and reset both timers.

`PlayerSessionSnapshot` now exposes detached frozen health/stamina/mana values, both timers, and whether the last movement tick actually sprinted. Existing DEV-only diagnostics consume this snapshot. Shell help, README, and startup instructions document Left Shift and current regeneration behavior.
