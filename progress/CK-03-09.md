## Result

`CombatSession` now owns sword state/runtime IDs, the physical damage resolver, target health, and one stable event collector. `PlayerSession` passes the live post-look/post-collision pose and actual command/tick to combat after movement/dash/sprint costs and before the one regeneration step. Combat adopts stamina and regeneration timers from the existing player pool. Active-hit resolution queries the current Grid and living actor colliders; immutable damage results update the actor records, and event batches use the actual simulation tick. World resets replace actors/resolver while retaining the HUD subscriber collector and issuing unique runtime IDs.

The guarded DEV training installation accepts a separate immutable Grid plus a validated starting pose. FloorSession renders and owns the Grid world, reports diagnostic mode and actual occupancy, supersedes pending floor loads, and retires the diagnostic render when generated loading succeeds. Player collision, target render position, and melee line-of-sight use the same Grid. The native fixture uses only the accepted training fixture's initial Grid/pose/target data; live combat queries and health are owned by CombatSession. The existing pixel-textured target and sword viewmodel are reused. Production can render the native sword/HUD in generated empty rooms but has no training controls or diagnostic global.

Pause, blur, hidden state, pointer-lock loss, editable focus, loading/replacement, and teardown clear sword anticipation immediately without synthesizing release. Camera-relative sword updates follow camera interpolation each frame. The HUD receives current resources and sword state and advances feedback only by simulated fixed-step seconds; pauses freeze those timers. The training toolbar is DEV-only and its listeners and DOM are retired on teardown.
