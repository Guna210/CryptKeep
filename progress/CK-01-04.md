## Scope and result

Added deterministic role assignment for connected floor geometry. `assignRoles(plan: ConnectedFloorPlan): RoleFloorPlan` copies and validates the floor plan and edge paths, then selects roles using actual cardinally reachable walkable cells. It never carves or resizes occupancy. Input room and edge records and occupancy values are preserved in detached, frozen output records. No app wiring, renderer, retry loop, fallback, enemies, hazards, content, or gate placeholders were added.

Changed paths: `src/dungeon/roles.ts`, `src/dungeon/roles.test.ts`, and this handoff. No other files were intentionally changed.

## Exported records and geometry

- `RoleFloorPlan extends ConnectedFloorPlan` with `roles`, `spawnPads`, `reservedPaths`, and `reservedCells`.
- `roles` contains `entryRoomId`, `bossRoomId`, `exitRoomId`, the `entry`, `approach`, `boss`, `reward`, and `exit` cell markers, and half-open `arena` bounds (`x`, `z`, `width`, `height`). Boss and exit room IDs both refer to the arena room.
- `spawnPads` contains four `{ kind, center, cells }` records for entry, boss, reward, and exit. Each pad has its center and its row-major 3×3 neighborhood; pads are within their room and mutually disjoint (Chebyshev center separation at least 3). Arena minimum is 7×7 wholly walkable cells inside the boss room. Entry selection requires a walkable 5×5 clearance rectangle in its room.
- `reservedPaths` contains four ordered cardinal walkable paths: entry→approach, approach→boss, boss→reward, and reward→exit. `approach` is a reachable boundary cell of the boss room, not another spawn pad. `reservedCells` is the deduplicated union of the whole arena rectangle, critical paths, and pad cells, sorted by row then column.
- A dedicated stream derived with domain `room-roles` and source `JSON.stringify([generatorVersion, floorNumber, "roles"])` chooses among safe entry cells and equal-distance farthest boss-room candidates. Rooms are sorted by stable string ID for selection, and BFS uses fixed east/west/south/north ordering.
- Malformed plans/edges reject with clear type/range errors. Plans with fewer than three rooms, no safe entry, unreachable arena topology, no 7×7 all-walkable boss-room rectangle, or insufficient separated pad geometry reject with `RangeError` for CK-01-05 to retry.
