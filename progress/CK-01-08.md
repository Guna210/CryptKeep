The app now generates a real floor 1 overview from the editable seed form. Each replacement is staged before the prior rendered floor is disposed; the single canvas, renderer, resize observer and shared material library stay alive for the session. The overview camera frames the complete grid, hides the ceiling in this view, and places the compact brand/status/seed controls and marker legend at the edge. Loading, recoverable seed/build failures, unsupported WebGL 2, and retry are represented in the actual UI.

`FloorSession` owns its generated/rendered floor, one shared `MaterialLibrary`, fixed-step clock and `EventCollector` subscription boundary. Requests are tokenized; overlapping requests are latest-wins, and pending work cannot publish after disposal. Failed replacement preserves the previous floor. Session lifecycle events are emitted once at their owning batch/tick. Hidden/blur pauses reset clock baselines on resume. The renderer context is retired only by final app teardown.

Development diagnostics add a detached, recursively frozen plain snapshot of lifecycle, normalized campaign seed, floor seed/number/hash, dimensions, room/walkable counts, role markers, generation attempts/fallback, current floor count, tick, interpolation alpha, and dropped time. It provides no mutation methods. Production builds omit the diagnostics global and retain working seed controls.

The controller accepts injected generation, library/floor construction, and frame-yield functions. Unit tests exercise deterministic plan hash/role data, distinct seeds, replacement disposal, retained floor after generator failure, invalid input before work, latest-request and disposal cancellation, tick bounds/pause baseline/lifecycle event uniqueness, and single library disposal.
