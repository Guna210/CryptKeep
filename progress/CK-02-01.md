## Interfaces and behavior

- `createPlayerState(floor, suppliedPose?)` returns `{ state, repaired }`. It keeps a supplied finite X/Z position and optional finite yaw/pitch only when the complete 0.28 m circle clears actual walkable occupancy and grid bounds. Invalid/missing pose repairs to the generated entry cell center; that fallback is checked and throws if unsafe.
- Wall-corner clearance uses a small coordinate-scale-aware floating-point tolerance so a mathematical tangent remains clear despite subtraction rounding; meaningful overlap still triggers repair.
- `PlayerState` starts with zero velocity, radius 0.28 m, camera height/Y 1.6 m, health 100/100, stamina 100/100, mana 60/60. Yaw and pitch are radians; yaw zero faces -Z. Records contain no browser or Three.js objects.
- `createResource`, `setResource`, and `adjustResource` preserve finite `[0, maximum]` bounds. `spendResource` returns a discriminated success/failure result; nonpositive or nonfinite costs fail without changing the resource, and insufficient spending leaves it unchanged.
- Resource helpers remain generic over a bounded resource maximum for later regeneration and resource extensions. No regeneration or inventory placeholders were added.
