# CK-03-06 — Create sword viewmodel and attack animation

**Builder:** `/root/ck_03_06_builder` (`gpt-6-luna`)
**Status:** Preliminary source submitted for hosted browser verification; not yet independently reviewed or accepted.

## Result

Added an original first-person sword model with a small generated 16×16 RGBA DataTexture atlas and nearest sampling. The steel blade, brass crossguard and pommel, wrapped grip, and rune share a few materials and five meshes. It attaches in camera-local coordinates, so yaw/pitch presentation follows the supplied camera. The update adapter reads immutable `SwordState`; it never invokes combat, writes weapon state, or owns the world renderer, scene, camera, or DOM.

The pose derives from anticipation/windup/active/recovery phase and elapsed simulation seconds. Charge builds during anticipation and lights the rune. Light/heavy attacks have distinct slash travel and scale; heavy has its own longer sweep. The slash starts when the state enters `active`, the same state boundary that emits the simulation hit request. Recovery eases back to the idle transform, and cancellation returns idle without glow. `attach`, `update`, `detach`, and repeated `dispose` are safe; disposal releases only viewmodel geometries, materials, texture, and root.

The browser fixture is isolated under `tests/harness/` and injects documented sword-state snapshots for rendering. It is not imported by the production campaign. Its Playwright case saves the tap-active and full-charge images at a 480×270 logical canvas, checks browser page/console errors, and confirms model disposal leaves the caller's camera/scene while renderer disposal retires its own context.
