## Result

Added an original first-person sword model with a small generated 16×16 RGBA DataTexture atlas and nearest sampling. The steel blade, brass crossguard and pommel, wrapped grip, and rune share a few materials and five meshes. It attaches in camera-local coordinates, so yaw/pitch presentation follows the supplied camera. The update adapter reads immutable `SwordState`; it never invokes combat, writes weapon state, or owns the world renderer, scene, camera, or DOM.

The pose derives from anticipation/windup/active/recovery phase and elapsed simulation seconds. Charge builds during anticipation and lights the rune. Light/heavy attacks have distinct slash travel and scale; heavy has its own longer sweep. The slash starts when the state enters `active`, the same state boundary that emits the simulation hit request. Recovery eases back to the idle transform, and cancellation returns idle without glow. `attach`, `update`, `detach`, and repeated `dispose` are safe; disposal releases only viewmodel geometries, materials, texture, and root.

The first hosted builder check, run `37580357082` job `112658359369` on candidate `acf5d5c`, passed typecheck, app/tool tests, build, and the 20 existing browser cases, but the new viewmodel browser case failed because the renderer's camera is not a child of its scene. Both uploaded tap/charge screenshots were inspected and showed a blank dark canvas. The original unit fixture had parented its camera into the scene, masking this traversal defect. The corrected model inserts its own root directly into the supplied scene and converts its camera-space pose into world coordinates on each update. It leaves camera parentage/siblings intact and removes only its root on detach/dispose. The unit case covers an unparented camera; the browser case checks nonzero rendered draw calls and preserves camera/context disposal assertions.

The corrected candidate `a36a13f8d5c17a44991262ab05b1ec1e4f3886b7` passed hosted run `37580926939`, job `112660163954`: typecheck, 161 app tests, tooling verification, build, and all 21 Chromium browser cases. The tap image shows a clear, pixel-textured full sword silhouette in the lower-right/center view; the full-charge image has a distinct diagonal pose and a bright blue rune. Neither screenshot is blank or clipped. The browser case reported no page or console errors and confirmed context disposal. The one coordinated repair now computes `rootRemoved` from `!renderer.scene.children.includes(model.root)` after repeated model disposal, preserving camera parentage and renderer context-retirement checks.
