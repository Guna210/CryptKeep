# CK-00-04 — Implement fixed-step clock

**Status: ACCEPTED by the main orchestrator after independent round-1 PASS.** Builder: GPT-6 Luna `/root/ck_00_04_builder`. Baseline: `81809d664e6fde4caa3c21ef83ddd034e906a326` (master). The accepted CK-00-01 handoff and the sequenced CK-00-03 handoff were present at start.

Implemented a standalone `FixedStepClock` in `src/core/clock.ts`. Its public contract accepts monotonic timestamps in milliseconds and returns the available fixed-tick count, a finite interpolation fraction, and dropped time in seconds. It runs at 60 Hz, caps total accumulated time to 0.1 seconds and each advance to six ticks, discards overflow, and reports that discarded duration. The first valid sample primes the baseline. `pause()` stops advancement; `resume(timestampMs)` clears accumulated time and establishes a fresh baseline. Nonfinite and backwards samples are ignored without changing the last valid baseline. The module has no browser, renderer, or gameplay dependencies.

`src/core/clock.test.ts` covers common 30/60/120/144 Hz schedules and irregular sub-100 ms intervals, first-sample and exact-tick boundaries, interpolation, a one-second stall, total-accumulator cap, overflow reporting and no retained backlog, pause/resume after a long hidden interval, repeated pause/resume, and invalid/backwards samples. README and startup instructions now describe the first Vitest suite and clarify that the clock is not yet connected to gameplay.
