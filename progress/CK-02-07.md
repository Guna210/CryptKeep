## Result

Added Space as a semantic dash pressed edge for captured active gameplay. Repeated DOM keydown events are ignored, held Space cannot repeat an activation, and an old input cancellation does not veto a genuinely fresh edge. An accepted dash pays 25 stamina once, records the spend through the existing stamina regeneration timer, and starts an immutable 0.22 second, 10 m/s movement with a 0.8 second cooldown and a 0.1 second evasion interval. The displacement is capped at 2.2 m; the final tick uses only the remaining duration. Direction is normalized from local movement axes and rotated by activation yaw, with yaw-forward as the no-input fallback; later mouse look does not redirect the dash.

Dash overrides walking and sprint for its tick. It uses the accepted swept circle collision query. Any contact cancels dash/evasion at the first contact center and discards the solver's remaining wall-slide result. The dash velocity is zeroed when contact or duration ends. Cooldown advances only on active fixed simulation ticks, including after dash motion ends; pause/blur/lock loss cancels active dash and evasion, retains the committed stamina cost and cooldown, and freezes that cooldown until play resumes. A successful floor load resets the dash state.

The detached player snapshot exposes `dash` and `evading` for later combat integration. No damage pipeline or blanket invulnerability was added. The shell help, README and startup instructions describe Space dash and its limits.

## APIs and units

- `createDashState()` returns a plain frozen state with `active`, `remainingSeconds`, `cooldownRemainingSeconds`, `evasionRemainingSeconds` and locked world-space `direction` in metres/second axes.
- `activateDash(state, stamina, pressed, yawRadians, axes)` returns `{ state, stamina, accepted }`; rejected edges preserve stamina. The caller gates it on captured active play and passes only a sampled fresh pressed edge.
- `advanceDash(state, dtSeconds)` uses simulation seconds and returns `{ state, displacement, evading }`. `evading` describes the interval consumed by this step; a 1e-10 second tolerance expires timer residue at boundaries.
- `resolveDashCollision(step, CircleMoveResult)` returns the first-contact center when present, cancels dash/evasion and sets zero velocity; without contact it keeps the sweep result and dash velocity unless dash duration ended.
- `cancelDash(state)` ends movement and evasion while retaining cooldown.
- `PlayerSessionSnapshot.dash` and `.evading` are read-only presentation state. No writable time or simulation diagnostic was added.
