# CK-03-05 — Implement sword charge and interruption

**Builder:** `/root/ck_03_05_builder` (GPT-6 Luna)
**Status:** Accepted and pushed after independent round-one repaired-snapshot PASS.

## Result

Extended the existing pure sword adapter with release-driven charge. A hold under 0.25 simulation seconds commits the unchanged light attack (18 damage, 10 stamina, 0.06 s windup, 0.12 s active, 0.30 s recovery). A hold at or above 0.25 seconds commits heavy. Heavy power uses `q = clamp((heldSeconds - 0.25) / 0.95, 0, 1)`, with damage `30 + 24q` and stamina `18 + 12q`; heavy timing is 0.10 s windup, 0.16 s active, and 0.50 s recovery. Reach and arc remain unchanged for the later query layer.

Threshold selection happens on ordered release edges at tick start, using compensated anticipation-time accumulation through prior ticks. Comparison is strict: representable durations below 0.25 seconds stay light, exactly 0.25 seconds selects heavy, and repeated 60 Hz ticks accumulate to exact tested boundaries (15 ticks at threshold, 72 ticks at full charge). Holds never fire automatically and power caps at 1.2 seconds.

`SwordState` now exposes committed kind, damage, and timing for later viewmodel readers. `isSwordCharging` and `swordChargeFraction` expose charge without callbacks or renderer state. The adapter still emits one typed attack request at active onset. The dispatcher allocates unique attack IDs and owns stamina commitment. A failed release returns idle and emits no hit; it never falls back from heavy to light. Explicit cancellation clears anticipation or committed weapon state without synthesizing release; already committed stamina remains spent. `InputSampler.clearInput` discards stale edges; the dispatcher cancels the old state, then preserves fresh ordered edges supplied after that clear.

## Interfaces and limits

- Readers: `SwordState.committedKind`, `.committedDamage`, `.committedTiming`, plus `isSwordCharging(state)` and `swordChargeFraction(state)`.
- Attack requests continue to use `kind`, `damage`, `attackId`, and `costCommitmentId` from the existing typed contract.
- Damage is requested only on entering the active window, as documented by CK-03-04; target entry during later active ticks is not queried here.
- Guard/parry, rendering, shared resource regeneration integration, and combat target queries remain later-task responsibilities.
