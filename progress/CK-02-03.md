# CK-02-03 — Implement swept circle/grid collision

**Builder/model:** `/root/ck_02_03_builder` / GPT-6 Luna

## Result and scope

Added a pure world-X/Z swept-circle resolver over actual `Grid` tile occupancy. It reports corrected position, applied displacement, collision contacts with outward normals, and blocked-axis flags. The default radius is the player state's 0.28 m radius. Invalid/nonfinite arguments and a starting circle that overlaps solids or extends outside the map fail explicitly; the resolver never repairs or teleports an invalid start. Only these files are owned: `src/player/collision.ts`, `src/player/collision.test.ts`, and this handoff. No Git operations were performed.

## Interface and guarantees

- `moveCircleOnGrid(grid, start, displacement, radius?)` accepts metres in the same X/Z frame as the floor grid and returns an immutable `CircleMoveResult`.
- Each tile is tested as its actual solid rectangle expanded by the circle radius, preserving rounded tile corners. Face and corner intersections are swept continuously over the full requested segment. The map perimeter also constrains the circle center by its radius.
- At most 4 contact/slide iterations run. Each pass checks at most the grid's 80×80 cells; leftover motion after the contact budget is discarded. Thus even an extremely long finite request cannot tunnel or cause an unbounded distance-proportional loop.
- At each hit, inward movement is removed from the remaining displacement and tangential movement continues. Motion away from or tangent to a contact does not create a blocking hit. Exact tangency is accepted for starts with a small coordinate-scale floating-point tolerance; meaningful overlap is rejected.
- `blockedX`/`blockedZ` are true when a contact normal constrains the corresponding axis. The controller can use contacts/flags to stop blocked velocity; this task does not mutate player state or commit candidate locomotion state.

## Limits

The resolver operates on a fixed static tile grid; moving actors and decorative scene geometry are outside its collision model. The iteration budget bounds work and favors safety by discarding any unresolved residual displacement at the cap. Contact axis flags identify constrained components, while the returned normals preserve the actual corner direction for future controller response.
