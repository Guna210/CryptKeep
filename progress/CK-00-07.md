Implemented a standalone typed `EventCollector` in `src/core/events.ts`, starting with session-ready, session-paused, session-resumed and session-disposed lifecycle events. Callers explicitly open a finite integer tick with `beginTick`, append events with `emit`, and consume an ordered immutable batch with `flush`. Each event is copied into a frozen primitive-only record with its tick; the batch array is frozen too. No app wiring, browser listeners, or gameplay stubs were added.

Subscriptions receive each flushed batch in registration order. Delivery uses a snapshot: unsubscribing during delivery takes effect on the next batch, and subscription during delivery is rejected. Callback failures are gathered while all remaining subscribers still run, then reported together as `AggregateError`; the current batch remains consumed and the next tick can proceed. Collector mutation during delivery (including recursive emit/flush and dispose) is rejected. Unsubscribe and dispose are idempotent, disposal clears subscriptions and pending state, and all later collector operations except repeated dispose fail explicitly.

`src/core/events.test.ts` covers ordered multi-tick delivery, frozen snapshots and mutation attempts, stable subscriber delivery across unsubscribe, subscriber failure isolation, invalid/missing tick boundaries, recursive mutation rejection, and idempotent cleanup/use-after-dispose behavior.
